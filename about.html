WELCOME TO 4OOM

4OOM is a retro-style forum inspired by the golden era of the internet (Web 1.0, GeoCities, Windows 95). We are a small community of people who miss the old web — the one with guestbooks, marquee tags, pixel art, and genuine human connection.


📜 RULES

  • Be nice to each other.
  • No spam, no bots, no illegal content.
  • Keep it retro. This is a place for nostalgia.
  • Have fun and express yourself!

🛠 HOW IT WORKS

This site is built entirely on Cloudflare Workers and D1 Database. It is a fully serverless, anonymous-friendly forum. You can log in via GitHub to get your custom nickname, avatar, and role.
