📜 4OOM FORUM RULES

1. Respect and Freedom: Be human. No insults, bullying, threats, or hate speech. Freedom of speech doesn't mean freedom from consequences. Argue with arguments, not with people.

2. Content: No illegal content (NSFL, weapons, etc.). If you post NSFW (18+), mark it in the title. No spam, ads, or massive piracy.

3. Tech & Security: Don't break the forum (no hacking, DDoS, exploits). Don't upload files larger than 5 MB to the database. One person - one account.

4. Moderation: Admins (Creator, Mod, Editor) can delete posts and issue bans. Don't argue with moderation in threads - write to a mod in chat. Don't impersonate admins.

5. Chat: The chat is a smoking room. Flood with images/music is ok, but respect applies. No spam to inflate the "online" count.

⚠️ Sanctions: 1st - warning. 2nd - 24h ban. 3rd - 7d ban. Severe violations (NSFL, hacking, harassment) - permanent ban.

The main rule: Don't be an asshole. This forum exists for the pleasure and nostalgia of the old internet. If you want to contribute - share music, images, create threads. If you want to start a fight - go to Twitter.

Have a pleasant stay!
